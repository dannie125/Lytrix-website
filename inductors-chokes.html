Magnetics for high-density compute

AI Server & Data Center Power

AI computing and high-density data-center infrastructure place increasing demands on power-conversion systems. Lytrix supports inductive and magnetic components for server power supplies, rack-level power systems and related high-current power-conversion applications.

From AC input filtering and PFC stages to high-current DC power conversion, requirements are reviewed against electrical, thermal and mechanical constraints. This includes AI server and GPU server power applications, with component selection evaluated for each design.

High-Current Power Inductors

Power inductors for high-current conversion stages where electrical performance, thermal behavior, size and efficiency must be balanced according to the application.

Potential applications

DC/DC conversion, high-current power rails, server power supplies, rack power systems, power modules and high-density power electronics.

RFQ parameters

Inductance, rated and peak current, saturation-current requirement, DC resistance, operating temperature and dimensions.

Flat-Wire Inductors

Flat-wire winding structures can support high-current and compact power applications where winding resistance, thermal performance and mechanical constraints are important design considerations.

Potential applications

High-density power conversion, server power supplies, DC/DC converters, power modules and high-current power stages.

RFQ parameters

Winding arrangement, DC resistance, thermal conditions, mounting and available space. Winding suitability depends on the design.

PFC Inductors

PFC inductors for power-factor-correction stages in AC-input power systems, including server and industrial power supplies.

Potential applications

AC/DC power supplies, server PSUs, high-power power supplies and power-conversion equipment.

RFQ parameters

Inductance, RMS current, peak current, switching frequency, core material, temperature requirement and mechanical dimensions.

Common-Mode Chokes

Common-mode chokes support conducted-noise suppression in high-frequency power-conversion and power-input applications.

Potential applications

AC input filtering, DC power filtering, server power supplies, power shelves, rack power systems and industrial power electronics.

RFQ parameters

Current, impedance requirement, noise frequency range, insulation and mounting. Review the equipment-level conducted-noise requirement.
