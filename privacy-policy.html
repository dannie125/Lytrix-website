Website & inquiry information

Privacy Policy

How this website handles browsing information and requests for quotation.

Browsing this website

This website does not include analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts. The hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website.

Submitting an inquiry

When you submit the inquiry form, the contact details and project requirements you enter are transmitted to Web3Forms, our form-processing provider, for delivery to our sales team. This may include your name, company, business email, phone number, business line, part number, quantity, application, message and any optional material, magnetic core, inductor, EMI filter, controller, component-sourcing or excess-inventory details you provide. These may include electrical specifications, firmware requirements, manufacturer, MPN, packaging, date code, condition, location, certification requirements, annual volume, target price or delivery date.

The form sends this information only when you choose Submit Inquiry. This website does not store form entries in cookies or browser storage. Your browser may independently retain entries through its autofill features.

How we use your information

Lytrix Technologies LLC uses the information you provide to review and respond to your inquiry and coordinate the supply support you request. Share only information needed for your project. You can email drawings or other supporting files separately when needed.

Service providers

Web3Forms processes submissions to provide form delivery and spam protection. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for details about its handling of information. Email providers also process messages delivered to our team or sent directly by you.

Privacy questions

Contact contact@ly-trix.com with questions about information you have shared or to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.
